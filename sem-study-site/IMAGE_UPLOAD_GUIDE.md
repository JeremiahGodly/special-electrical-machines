## How to open the site
1. Open `sem-study-site/index.html` in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox)
2. Images will auto-appear once you upload them to the `images/` folder
3. Click any question card to expand/collapse the answer
4. Hover over **green highlighted terms** to see their definitions
